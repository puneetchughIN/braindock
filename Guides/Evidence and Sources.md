# Evidence and Sources

Keep sources with the project whose conclusions they support. Preserve originals; make an editable derivative when necessary and label it clearly. Store an original once and link to it.

A source register records relative location, role, origin, supported dates, and known gaps. Checksums can help establish file identity, but do not establish truth.

Distinguish direct evidence, inference, and hypothesis. Preserve conflicting evidence rather than averaging away differences. Use the precision supported: a known year is better than an invented date.

For web evidence, record the title, URL, access date, claim supported, and whether a durable snapshot exists. External links may change.

Generated reports, slides, and PDFs are outputs. Identify their source records and snapshot date. Update the canonical record before regenerating a deliverable.
